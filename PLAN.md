# Nature de cheveux — Research & Plan

> **Nature de cheveux** is an independent organic hair salon (*coiffeur bio*) and plant-colour specialist (*coloriste végétal*). It is at **3 bis rue du Calvaire, 35510 Cesson-Sévigné**, in the old village centre of a Rennes Métropole commune just east of Rennes. It is **not in Rennes itself**, although it markets itself as "votre coiffeur bio sur Rennes – Cesson Sévigné". **Yohann Hubert** founded it in **2011** and still runs it, with a small team of 3 to 5 people. The colour is made **only from plant powders and water** (henné, indigo, rhubarbe, avoine, guimauve, curcuma) and **prepared in front of the client**. The salon also offers mineral colour and clay lightening, dry cutting, and a recognised skill with **curly and textured hair**. It sells organic, bulk and solid products in the salon. The sustainability record is unusually deep:
> - Ecocert "Établissement bien-être durable" **3 étoiles** (2024), which the salon presents as a first;
> - Prix RSE de l'UNEC 2024;
> - Enercoop renewable electricity;
> - hair donated to Fake Hair Don't Care and recycled with Capillum;
> - a sponsored beehive;
> - laundry detergent made from fireplace ash.
>
> Clients describe the salon as **douceur, écoute, cocon**: tea and honey while the colour sets, a free massage at the shampoo, a heated cap, and a quiet room. The visual identity is more colourful than a typical "eco beige" salon. The calligraphic logo flows like a strand of hair in teal, navy, raspberry, violet and leaf green, and a watercolour mural of a woman with red lips covers a wall inside. The salon's own signature line is **"Naturellement VOTRE coiffure."**

## 2. Story & history

- **2011: founding.** Yohann Hubert registered the business on 22 Nov 2011 at 3 bis rue du Calvaire, Cesson-Sévigné. He chose a sole-trader structure under his own name, with the enseigne *Nature de cheveux*. Name and address have not changed since.
  - The website has been online since 2011.
  - Before 2011 he worked in an earlier salon with his employee Mélanie (Enercoop; the salon is never named). One Google review says a client followed him from that earlier job in Rennes to Cesson.
- **Eco from day one.** "Depuis 2011, Nature de cheveux est engagé dans la coiffure durable pour donner du sens à notre métier en plaçant l'humain, l'écologie et la qualité au cœur de notre démarche en travaillant avec des fournisseurs responsables et éthiques." (brochure)
  - Enercoop quotes Yohann (26 Jun 2024; **now verified** from the Wayback snapshot http://web.archive.org/web/20260214200441/https://www.enercoop.fr/blog/actualites/bretagne/nature-de-cheveux-a-choisi-enercoop): « Nature de Cheveux est un salon de coiffure situé à Cesson-Sévigné (35) et créé en 2011. Nous sommes engagé·es depuis la création du salon pour une pratique durable de notre métier en plaçant en priorité l'humain, l'écologie et la qualité. Spécialisé·es en coloration végétale et en coupe, nous nous formons régulièrement avec les meilleur·es dans leurs domaines pour offrir à nos client·es une expérience unique, personnalisée et qualitative. »
  - The washable colour papers are « les mêmes depuis l'ouverture en 2011 ! » (Enercoop, verified).
  - More from the same interview (verified, usable as « selon le salon »):
    - « réduire au maximum l'impact de notre métier sur notre belle planète »
    - « offrir à notre clientèle des prestations de haute coiffure dans une ambiance chaleureuse, décontractée et apaisante »

## 9. Design direction

**Design read:** a premium local-service site for loyal, eco-minded women in the east of the Rennes area (and their children), with a botanical-editorial, colour-craft visual language. It leans toward vanilla Vite + TS, native CSS tokens (or Tailwind v4), GSAP ScrollTrigger + Lenis, and a light WebGL/SVG pigment effect.
**Dials** (from `design-taste-frontend` and `ui-ux-pro-max`): VARIANCE **6**, MOTION **8**, DENSITY **3** (spacious).

**Rejected defaults, and why:**
- The `ui-ux-pro-max` "Beauty/Spa" database result (pink #EC4899 + lavender #8B5CF6 on #FDF2F8, Lora/Raleway) is the generic spa template.
- The market report's palette (oat cream #F6F0E6, clay ochre, terracotta, plum) is the "warm paper + clay + oxblood" family that `design-taste-frontend` bans as the premium-consumer default.

Neither is *this* salon. Its real colours are the **logo's cool, saturated inks** (teal, navy, raspberry, violet, leaf green), its watercolour mural and its purple-blue floral window film. Warm earth tones (henné, curcuma, argile) appear only as **content**: the swatches of what plants actually produce, never as the UI chrome.

### 9.1 Concept: **« Poudres & eau »**
*Every colour on this site is made the way the salon makes it: plant powder plus water, stirred in front of you, then laid on a single strand of hair.*

The site's spine is **one continuous strand of hair**. It is an SVG path that grows out of the calligraphic swash of the logo's "N" and runs down the page as the scroll-progress line. As it passes each section it **takes the dye** of that section's plant: henné copper, indigo blue-black, rhubarbe gold, curcuma warmth. By the footer the visitor has scrolled a whole *nuancier*.

### 9.2 Personality keywords
douce · experte · engagée · lumineuse · artisanale · à l'écoute · un brin espiègle (the "En France et sur la planète !" voice) · *pas* clinique · *pas* beige

### 9.4 Type pairing
The serif is justified because the brand's own mark is a calligraphic, high-contrast serif. The brochure supplies the typewriter element.

- **Display: Bodoni Moda** (Google Fonts, variable opsz, with italics).
  - It is the high-contrast, calligraphic Didone that echoes the logo's thick/thin swash.
  - Use the *italic* for emphasis inside the same family. For example, set "Naturellement *VOTRE* coiffure.": the original uses capitals, so keep « VOTRE » in caps and italic.
  - Leave 1.1 line-height under italic descenders.
- **Text / UI: Hanken Grotesk.** A warm, humanist grotesk, readable at 16–18 px. It is not Inter.
- **Label / "fiche" voice: Courier Prime.** A typewriter face, a direct nod to the brochure's typewriter headings. Use it **only** for diagnostic cards, price tags (*fiche tarif*), plant labels in the herbarium, and the dates in the journal. Never use it for body text.
- **Not used:** Fraunces, Instrument Serif, Lora/Raleway, Cormorant (Il Colorista and Kalm Moments already use it), Poppins (the booking platform's font).
- Self-host the fonts with `@font-face` + `font-display: swap`, and subset them for French (accents, « », non-breaking spaces).

### 9.5 Imagery approach (we may not use their photos)
- **No scraped salon photos, no logo PNG, no copy of the mural** until the owner gives permission (TODO). Everything is original:
  1. **A herbarium plate per plant.** Original line drawings (SVG, hand-quality strokes, not stock leaves) of henné, indigo, rhubarbe, avoine, guimauve and curcuma, plus clay, seaweed and organic oils.   2. **Procedural hair strands.** SVG or canvas strands with dye gradients for the *nuancier* and the gallery. There are three textures: *lisse*, *ondulé*, *bouclé*. Curly strands get real coil geometry, because curls matter to this clientele.

### 9.6 Layout ideas

### 9.7 Signature interactions (unique to this business)
1. **« Le bol »: mix your own plant colour (main signature).**
   - **What it is.** A ceramic bowl in a small canvas or SVG scene, beside six powder jars (henné, indigo, rhubarbe, avoine, guimauve, curcuma).
   - **Adding powder.** Tap a jar to add a spoonful: a powder puff, then particles settle in the bowl.
   - **Adding water.** Pour water with a jug button or a drag.
   - **Stirring.** Make circular pointer drags (or press a "Mélanger" button for keyboard users, or hold the space bar). The paste blends in OKLCH from the swatch weights, and a strand of hair beside the bowl takes the tint in real time.
   - **Output.** A typewriter *fiche* names the nearest *nuancier* shade (mock name) and states, word for word:
     > « Résultat indicatif : chaque coloration végétale est unique. Pour une première coloration, un diagnostic au salon ou par téléphone est indispensable. »

     It is followed by **one CTA, "Appeler le salon"** (`tel:+33299839009`).
   - **Honesty rules.**
     - Proportions are a toy, not salon recipes.
     - If the mix drifts toward impossible results, say gently that plant colour does not make pinks, blues or polar blonds (§3.4).
     - Results are announced in an `aria-live` region.
2. **« 10 cm »: the hair-donation ruler (secondary).**
   - **What it is.** A strand of hair hangs over a ruler, with a pair of scissors (drag on mobile, arrow keys on desktop) that you move to set a cut length.
   - **Behaviour.**
     - Longer than **10 cm**, the cut lock flies to a "Fake Hair Don't Care" envelope, with copy about wigs for people in cancer treatment.
     - Shorter, it goes to "Capillum", which recycles cut hair (for oil-spill clean-up and agricultural mulch).
   - **Grounding.** The facts are exact, from the own site and UNEC.
3. **« Qui êtes-vous ? »: the visagiste *fiche*.** The salon's four real questions are shown one at a time, with tactile choice chips. The answers fill a typewriter diagnostic card ("Votre nature de cheveux") that ends with "Naturellement VOTRE coiffure." It does not store or send data; the card can be printed or copied.

### 9.8 Motion language
Stack (search and verify current docs and caniuse **before building**, as CLAUDE.md requires):
- GSAP + ScrollTrigger + SplitText (confirm the current licence)
- Lenis smooth scroll
- CSS scroll-driven animations (`animation-timeline: view()`) for light reveals, with an `@supports` fallback
- View Transitions API for cross-document page transitions (`@view-transition { navigation: auto; }`), with a fade fallback
- an optional tiny WebGL fragment shader (raw WebGL or OGL, lazy-loaded) for the pigment bloom, with an SVG-filter fallback

Animate only `transform`, `opacity`, and `clip-path` / filter sparingly. Do not mix Motion and GSAP.

- **Hero (asymmetric split).**
  - **Left:**
    - Headline « Naturellement VOTRE coiffure. », split by SplitText into letters that **"take colour"** one by one. Each letter drops from ink-grey into indigo as if dye were soaking up, with the italic VOTRE in framboise.
    - Sub-line of 20 words or fewer: « Coiffeur bio et coloriste végétal à Cesson-Sévigné, aux portes de Rennes. Plantes, eau et écoute, depuis 2011. »
    - CTAs: **Réserver** (SmartBooker) and **Appeler le salon**.
  - **Right:** a pigment bloom. Teal, indigo and henné ink diffuse slowly in "water", and the **strand** draws itself out of the bloom (SVG `stroke-dashoffset`), curling like the logo's N.
  - The pointer gently perturbs the pigment. The load sequence takes 1.6 s at most.
- **Scroll storytelling: « De la poudre à la mèche »** (Coloration page, and a short version on Home). A pinned section (`start: "top top"`, scrubbed) in six beats:
  1. powders (herbarium plates fan out)
  2. water poured (a ripple)
  3. paste stirred (the bowl rotates and the colour blends)
  4. application under the *charlotte chauffante*, with a tea and honey cup steaming in the corner
  5. rinse, with a massage at the shampoo (the indigo "rinse" wave)
  6. reveal, as the strand changes to its final shade

  Each beat has a single short caption with a real fact.
- **Nuancier**: a horizontal pan (pinned, `end: +=distance`, `scrub: 1`). The strands sway slightly with scroll velocity, clamped.
- **The strand as scroll progress**: a fixed thin SVG strand down the left edge (or the bottom edge on mobile) that fills, and **changes dye per section** through ScrollTrigger callbacks.
- **Page transitions (« le rinçage »)**: a wave of indigo water sweeps up with `clip-path`, rising over 420 ms and receding over 380 ms. It uses the View Transition API where supported; a simple cross-fade is the fallback.
- **Micro-interactions**:
  - buttons "soak" (a framboise fill rising from the bottom via clip-path) on hover/focus, and press to `scale(0.98)`
  - links underline with a tiny curling hair stroke
  - price *fiches* tilt slightly (2° max) on pointer devices only
  - the hours panel highlights today and shows **« Ouvert / Fermé maintenant »**, computed from the real hours (Europe/Paris)
  - the bee in the engagements bento follows a slow figure-eight path, once on enter and not in a loop
  - the Réserver button may be magnetic, on desktop pointer devices only
  - no custom cursor
- **Marquee**: at most one on the whole site. It runs on Home, slowly, with the plant names and their properties in Bodoni italic.
- **Reduced-motion fallback** (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, also for `saveData` and low `hardwareConcurrency`):
  - no Lenis, no pinning, no scrub, no shader
  - the hero shows a static watercolour still with the strand fully drawn and the headline already coloured
  - the process becomes six stacked illustrated steps
  - the *nuancier* becomes a native horizontal scroll-snap row
  - page transitions become an instant swap or a 150 ms opacity change
  - the bowl still works and updates the colour instantly
  - all content stays in the DOM, never only inside the canvas
